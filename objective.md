imp:SMOOTH AND GOOD LOOKING UI
A. Daily study planner
1. 5 Daily Study Tasks – Five task rows for every date.
2. Automatic Today's Date – Automatically identifies the current date.
3. Flexible Task Entry – Type your own aims or select from a custom dropdown list.
4. Automatic Task Numbering – Tasks numbered 1–5 automatically.
5. Weightage Dropdown – Select weightage from 1–10 for every entered task.
6. Compulsory Weightage – Every entered task must have a weightage.
7. Automatic Weight Normalisation – Redistributes entered task weights to total 100%.
8. Simple Completion System – Only ✓ in green and ✗ in red; no completion words.
9. Automatic Task Scoring – ✓ earns the task's normalised weight; ✗ earns zero.
10. Daily Productivity Percentage – Automatically calculated out of 100%, prominently displayed.
11. Daily Study Notes – One expandable notes cell per day.
12. Pending Task Management – Identify unfinished tasks and manually carry selected tasks to another date.
B. Monthly planner and calendar
1. Separate Monthly Worksheets – One monthly dashboard and one daily planner for each month.
2. Automatic Monthly Date Structure – All dates and weekdays generated automatically.
3. Monthly Calendar – Displays every date, including future dates.
4. Three-Level Productivity Heatmap – Easy-to-understand productivity colours.
5. Clickable Calendar Dates – Click any date to jump to its daily planner section.
C. Productivity graphs and statistics
1. Daily Productivity Line Graph – Tracks daily productivity throughout the month.
2. Weekly Productivity Bar Graph – Compares weekly average productivity, Monday–Sunday.
3. Monthly Productivity Bar Graph – Displays monthly productivity trends.
4. Overall Productivity Donut Chart – Visual representation of productivity.
5. Weekly Average Productivity – Automatically calculates weekly averages, excluding days with no tasks.
6. Monthly Average Productivity – Automatically calculates monthly averages.
